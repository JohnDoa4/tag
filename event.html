An evening in colour

Paint a fabric lampshade together at a shared table. No experience needed, just curiosity.

When
Sat 7 Nov, 15.00
Length
3 hours
Where
Partner café, Istanbul
Places
5 left of 12

About this evening

We begin with a short conversation about colour: what it does to a room, and what it does to us. Then we paint a fabric lampshade, each at our own pace, with the table to share and no one watching over your shoulder.

It is a slow evening on purpose. You leave with something of your own, and probably with a few new faces to say hello to next time.

Programme

  1. 15.00
    Arrive and settle

    Tea, a seat at the table, a first look at the colours.

  2. 15.20
    A short conversation about colour

    Ten minutes to warm up the room.

  3. 15.45
    Painting, at your own pace

    Your host is around to help, never to hurry you.

  4. 17.30
    Around the table

    We look at what everyone made and talk about it.

  5. 18.00
    Close

    The lampshade goes home with you once it is dry.

What to bring

  • Nothing at all. Materials, aprons and tea are included.
  • Comfortable clothes that can handle a little paint.
