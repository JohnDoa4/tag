How courses work

One price, one group, one series.

  1. Join the whole series

    You pay once for every meeting. No need to book each evening separately.

  2. Meet the same group

    The same people, evening after evening. Conversations get deeper when we know each other.

  3. Find it all in your account

    Every meeting appears under “My courses”, with dates, venue and what is coming up next.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What if I miss a meeting?

Life happens. We share short notes from the evening you missed, and the reading list stays open to you. Moving to another group is possible when places allow.

Do I need to prepare anything?

For reading courses we send the list in advance. For ceramics and other making courses, all materials are included.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, up to seven days before the first meeting. After that we’ll talk it through case by case. The full policy is on our terms page.
