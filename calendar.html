How booking works

Three small steps.

  1. Choose a gathering

    Pick a date that suits you. Every gathering page names the venue, the host and how many places are left.

  2. Reserve your place

    Free gatherings are confirmed straight away. Paid ones are confirmed after payment.

  3. We send the details

    You will get an email with the venue, the time and a reminder a day or two before.
