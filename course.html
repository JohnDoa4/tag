Who it’s for

For anyone who wants to read with company, without hurry and without homework guilt. You do not need to be a fast reader, or a regular one. You only need a little curiosity about what a sentence can do.

The meetings

  1. 03 Dec
    Why read slowly?

    We meet, set the pace and read the first pages aloud.

  2. 10 Dec
    The first page

    What does an opening promise, and what does it hide?

  3. 17 Dec
    A sentence, twice

    Rereading as a way of listening.

  4. 24 Dec
    Characters at the table

    Who do we side with, and why?

  5. 07 Jan
    What stays

    The things a book leaves with us after the last page.

  6. 14 Jan
    Closing evening

    A last conversation and a reading list for the winter.

What’s included

  • Six meetings of about two hours each.
  • The reading list, sent in advance.
  • Tea or coffee at the table.
  • A place in the same group from beginning to end.
