About the Akademi

One table.
Many possibilities.

“Space to think together.
Room to hear each other.”

Terapi Gibi Akademi is a meeting place at the intersection of psychology, art and human experience. We read, make things and leave room for a shared search for meaning. We meet in person at the cafés and art galleries we work with in Istanbul.
