Read · Think · Make

One table. Many possibilities.

Space to think together. Room to hear each other.

Terapi Gibi Akademi is a meeting place at the intersection of psychology, art and human experience. We read, make things and leave room for a shared search for meaning.

The name says it quietly: terapi gibi, “like therapy”. Not therapy itself, but something that feels a little like it: being heard, taking your time, finding the words.

We meet in person at the cafés and art galleries we work with in Istanbul.

What we believe

Three small convictions.

I

Slowness

A good conversation, like a good book, does not need to be finished quickly.

II

Curiosity

You do not need experience, only an open question and the wish to hear someone else’s.

III

Company

We think better together. A place at the table is the whole invitation.

Where we meet

Not our studio. Our hosts’ rooms.

We gather in cafés and galleries that share our spirit. Each gathering page names its venue.

Partner cafés

Warm rooms with long tables, where reading groups and conversations feel at home.

Partner galleries

Quiet spaces with good light for workshops and trainings.

Always in person

Every gathering is offline. You book online, then simply come.
