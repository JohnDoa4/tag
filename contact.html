Where we meet

Our gatherings take place in partner cafés and galleries in Istanbul. The exact venue is on each gathering’s page and in your confirmation email.

Quick answers

Before you write.

How do I book a place?

Open a gathering in the calendar and choose “Reserve a place”. Paid gatherings are confirmed once payment goes through.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Each gathering page says until when. You can also cancel from your account.

Do you have a studio I can visit?

No. We meet in partner cafés and galleries, and our registered address is a virtual office with no gatherings.
