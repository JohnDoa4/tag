Psychotherapy

Make space to listen to yourself.

A quiet starting point for learning about psychotherapy and understanding the process.

What to expect

Psychotherapy is a conversation with a trained professional, at a pace that is yours. It is not advice and it is not a quick fix. It is a place to say things out loud and to be heard.

This page is an introduction. It does not take appointments or payments, and the Akademi does not run a clinic. If you would like to talk, write to us and we will point you to a specialist.

Information only

No appointments, patient accounts or payments happen on this site.

Ways to work

Two ways to begin.

Individual

One to one, with someone who listens closely. The length and the rhythm are agreed together.

Group

A small circle of people with a shared theme, guided by a professional. Often a gentler way to start.

How to begin

Three quiet steps.

  1. Read about the process

    Take your time. There is no deadline for deciding.

  2. Write to us

    Ask anything: what to expect, what it might cost, whether it is right for you.

  3. We point you to a specialist

    We help you find the right person to talk to.

Questions

Things people often ask.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What is said in a session stays in the session. Your specialist will explain the details at the start.

How long does a session last?

Most sessions last about fifty minutes. Your specialist will tell you what to expect for your own situation.

What does it cost?

Fees are set by each specialist and shared before you begin. Nothing is paid through this website.

If you are in danger

This website is not a crisis service. If you or someone close to you is in danger, contact local emergency services immediately.
